## Executive summary

We now have two independent data sources shaping the plan: (1) two months of stalled LSA troubleshooting on JAC's existing accounts, and (2) 365 days of actual closed-job revenue data (63 jobs, $1.35M total). Together they change the shape of the strategy in three ways:

**1. This is fundamentally a Central Florida company.** 82% of revenue comes from the Orlando / Central FL metro; 10% from Tampa Bay; 8% from the Space Coast. The current website treats Tampa Bay and Central Florida as coequal divisions — that framing overweights Tampa relative to actual customers. **17 of the 28 cities currently listed as service areas produced zero jobs in the last 365 days**, which is doorway-page territory that quietly drags down the whole domain's quality signals.

**2. Four "should-be-easy" cities are leaking money.** Kissimmee, Winter Park, Sanford, and Lake Mary produced $232K in revenue over the last year yet have **soft local packs** — top-3 competitors with 8–120 reviews when JAC's Orlando GBP already has 145 reviews at 5★. JAC should already be ranking top-3 in these cities and isn't, almost certainly because Google's proximity signal isn't seeing the Orlando GBP as local to those satellites. This is the single biggest fixable opportunity in the plan.

**3. Paid Google Search Ads remains the workhorse.** After two months of LSA effort with no meaningful lead flow, we're not staking budget on LSA / Performance Max pay-per-lead unlocking. Google Search Ads (branded + non-branded) is the only channel we budget for; LSA and the New Method continue as background workstreams for possible upside.

**What that means, month 12:**
- **Base case (Search Ads only, LSA stays broken, Tier 1 SEO shipped):** ~30–55 leads/month at $250–430 per lead, ~$10–13K/month ad spend
- **Upside case (LSA/PMax comes online at some point or Tier 1 GBP work produces local-pack rankings):** ~55–100 leads/month at $170–290 per lead for similar spend
- **12-month total, base case:** ~250–450 leads on $100–125K ad spend, at an average of $280–460 per lead

The base case is meaningfully better than the prior version because the SEO/GBP work on the four soft-pack cities has real chance of producing organic + local-pack leads at near-zero incremental cost, on top of the paid channel.

## What we've learned from 2 months of LSA effort

1. **This is not an "administrative fix" scenario.** Simple causes (unaccepted terms, declined payment, expired license, obvious badge issue) would have been resolved by now. The block is more likely a **policy-, entity-, or GBP-link-level issue** requiring Tier-2 escalation over weeks.
2. **The PMax pay-per-lead migration is not a guaranteed reset.** Google's transition explicitly carries lead history, verification status, and account settings forward. Underlying account issues inherit.
3. **The industry-benchmark LSA cost per lead ($80–150) only applies once the account is actually serving.** JAC is not near that today. Reserving budget against those numbers would be irresponsible.
4. **Google Search Ads carries the plan.** It's a mature, well-instrumented channel we can predict cost per lead for. That's the workhorse.

## The revenue vs. coverage mismatch (site audit angle)

JAC's current site lists 28 service-area pages (14 Central FL + 14 Tampa Bay). The 365-day job data:

- **11 pages produced revenue** — 7 in Central FL (Orlando, Winter Park, Kissimmee, Sanford, Winter Garden, Winter Haven, Altamonte Springs) and 4 in Tampa Bay (Tampa, Largo, Plant City, New Port Richey vicinity).
- **17 pages produced zero jobs** in the last 365 days — this is textbook doorway-page risk. Google penalizes near-identical templated city pages that don't have real business behind them, and it drags the whole domain's quality signals down.

Two clean paths:
1. Keep the pages, heavily differentiate content per city (real photos of local work, real testimonials, actual crew coverage details). Expensive, slow.
2. **Retire the zero-revenue city pages, 301-redirect to their metro landing page, keep only the pages backed by actual revenue.** Fast, safe, better for site quality.

Recommend option 2 for the 17 zero-revenue pages while investing that saved time into content quality on the 11 that do produce.

## How we arrived at the numbers

Every projected number traces to one of these sources. Where we're extrapolating from industry benchmarks rather than JAC's own actuals, we say so.

### 1. Revenue tiers and city priority
- **Source:** JAC's own 365-day closed-job data (63 jobs, $1.35M revenue, provided September 2026).
- **Method:** grouped jobs by city + metro; computed revenue-per-city; cross-referenced with market volume and competitive position (below) to produce the tier system.
- **Why this matters:** revenue-anchored tiers are far more reliable than keyword-volume-anchored tiers. Search volume tells you where people are looking; revenue tells you where JAC is actually paid. They don't always match.

### 2. Market demand (search volumes)
- **Source:** Google Ads Keyword Planner via DataForSEO's `keywords_data/google_ads/search_volume/live` endpoint. Original pull August 14, 2026; supplemental Tier 3 / satellite-city pull September 15, 2026.
- **Keywords:** 15 core roofing service terms (roof repair, roof repair near me, roofer near me, roofing contractor, roof replacement, new roof, roof installation, roof leak repair, roof leak, storm damage roof repair, hail damage roof, emergency roof repair, shingle roof repair, tile roof repair, roof inspection).
- **Cities:** 28 initial cities + 14 supplemental cities revealed by the 365-day CSV as revenue-producing but not previously in the pipeline.
- **Long-tail multiplier:** DataForSEO keyword expansion at the primary city gave a 60-term filtered service-intent universe. Multiplier = (60-term total volume) ÷ (15 core-term total volume). Orlando: 7.13×. Tampa: 7.51×. Applied to core volumes to estimate the true universe of queries JAC would rank for.

### 3. Cost per click and cost per lead
- **Source:** DataForSEO Search Volume pulls return Google Ads' `low_top_of_page_bid` (position 3–4 proxy) and `high_top_of_page_bid` (position #1 proxy) per keyword. Volume-weighted per city.
- **CVR — conservative:** 3% first 90 days (before landing-page optimization), 4% thereafter. Sources: LocaliQ 2025 home-services benchmarks (roofing & gutters median CVR 3.7% — one of the lowest in home services because the decision requires trust proofs).
- **Impression share:** 30% early, rising toward 40% with account maturity (60–80% is aspirational and requires budget headroom we won't have in the first 6 months).
- **CTR:** 5% (home services average 4.8%).
- **Real-world margin:** Google's suggested bids typically settle 10–30% higher than winning CPCs for well-run accounts. We use suggested bids conservatively for planning.

### 4. Competitive position — local pack top-3 per city
- **Source:** DataForSEO's `serp/google/organic/live/advanced` local-pack results for money queries per city ("roof repair {city}"), August–September 2026.
- **JAC's GBPs verified:** DataForSEO `business_data/google/my_business_info/live` — JAC Builders of Orlando (145 reviews, 5.0★), JAC Builders, INC. Tampa (285 reviews, 4.9★).
- **Method:** for each Tier 1/2/3 city, extracted top-3 local-pack businesses' review counts + ratings. Cross-referenced against JAC's GBP to compute "review gap" and identify soft-pack opportunities.

### 5. LSA / Performance Max pay-per-lead cost per lead (upside case only)
- **Source:** Agency-community 2024–2026 benchmarks by trade. Roofing sits at $80–150 per LSA lead across most saturated U.S. metros when the account is serving. This is not Google-published; it's the consensus range observed by agencies running roofing LSA. Directional.
- **Applicability to JAC:** The base plan does not use these numbers. They appear only in the upside case if / when the LSA or new PMax pay-per-lead campaign actually starts producing leads at benchmark.

### 6. Ranking factors (LSA / Google Verified)
- **Source:** Community consensus 2025–2026 (see the LSA Lead Diagnostic reference doc in this project).
- **Weight order:** Responsiveness → Reviews (count × velocity × rating) → Proximity → Profile completeness → Lead history / booking outcomes → Bid/budget.

### 7. What's *not* in this plan (and how we'd get it)
- **JAC's current actual cost per lead** on any channel — replaces our industry benchmarks after month 1 of measurement
- **JAC's actual close rate and job value** — shifts reporting from cost per lead to cost per booked job
- **JAC's landing-page conversion baseline** — measured in week 1
- **The root cause of the LSA account issue** — comes out of Tier-2 support engagement, not from us or DataForSEO
- **The exact revenue impact of doorway-page retirement** — measured 60–90 days after retirement in Google Search Console
